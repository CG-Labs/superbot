AI teammates with their own computer

Your always-on AI team.

SuperBot gives every person a team of AI agents that keep working when you don't. They work on their own always-on computer, so they can browse, write, run tools and finish real tasks, then hand back to you when it needs a human.

Built to do the work, not just talk about it

  • A real computer, always on

    Your agents work on an always-on server with real apps and a real browser. Each one gets its own screen, and you can watch or take over at any time.

  • Personal and team bots

    Keep private bots for your own work, and share team bots so everyone builds on the same knowledge. You choose who can see and run each one.

  • Routines that run themselves

    Schedule briefings, reports, checks and follow-ups in plain words. Results land in your chat, and bots can ask each other for help.

  • You stay in control

    Risky actions wait for your approval. Every action is recorded in an audit log, and secrets are encrypted at rest.
