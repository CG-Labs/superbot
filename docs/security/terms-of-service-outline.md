| # | Section | What it should say | Product facts to check it against |
|---|---------|--------------------|-----------------------------------|
| 1 | Acceptance and parties | Who the agreement is with; business or consumer customers; authority to bind an organisation; order forms for Enterprise. | — |
| 2 | The Service | AI teammates (Bots) that run on a computer of their own, on the plan the customer buys. | Plans pick the model, not the customer ([ADR 0033](../adr/0033-tiers-model-routing.md)); one machine per user on managed plans ([ADR 0025](../adr/0025-control-plane-linode.md), [ADR 0065](../adr/0065-incus-container-machines.md)); a computer with a screen ([ADR 0003](../adr/0003-computer.md)). |

| 4 | Acceptable use | No unlawful, harmful or abusive use; no attacks on the Service or others; no use of Bots to break other services' terms; limits on automated scraping, spam and crypto mining **[legal review]**. | Bots act on the customer's instructions; risky actions wait for a person's approval by default, under a policy admins can change ([ADR 0007](../adr/0007-approvals.md)). |
| 5 | Responsibility for Bots' actions | The customer is responsible for what its Bots are asked to do and for approvals its people give; AI output can be wrong and must be checked. | Approvals and the policy editor ([ADR 0049](../adr/0049-policy-editor-in-the-apps.md)); the audit log records who approved what ([ADR 0019](../adr/0019-audit-actors.md)). |

| 8 | Fees, billing and plan changes | Prices per plan, billing period, taxes, renewals, upgrades and downgrades, what happens to the computer on a plan change, no free tier. | Stripe checkout and portal ([ADR 0042](../adr/0042-billing-checkout-portal-customers.md)); token metering ([ADR 0050](../adr/0050-billing-period-token-usage.md)); team plans ([ADR 0093](../adr/0093-team-billing.md)); resizing on a plan change ([ADR 0085](../adr/0085-resizing-machines-on-a-tier-change.md)); no free tier ([ADR 0068](../adr/0068-no-free-tier.md)). |
